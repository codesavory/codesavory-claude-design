The Codesavory mark is a ring that stays open on the right, like a letter C, with a saffron seed sitting in the opening. The ring is the studio; the seed is the thing it is waiting for. Products borrow it: Kickoff closes the ring as a match approaches.

Single-ink rules, because SVGs shown through an image cannot inherit colour:
- `mark-on-light.svg`: ring in `tide-600` (#234cba), seed in `saffron-400` (#ffcd29). For `surface` and `surface-raised` in the light theme and any white ground.
- `mark-on-dark.svg`: ring in `salt-50` (#f4f7fa), seed in `saffron-400`. For `surface`, `surface-inverse` and dark photography.
- `mark-mono-ink.svg`: both parts in `tide-950` (#081333). For one-colour print, stamps and favicons on light grounds. For a one-colour mark on dark, recolour the file to `salt-50`.
- `kickoff-icon-proposal.svg`: a proposed Kickoff app icon on `tide-800` with a `tide-700` track, a `saffron-400` arc at 70 percent and a `salt-50` seed. Flat, no gradient, so it can be rebuilt as layers in Icon Composer. It is a proposal, not shipped.

Clear space around the mark is the width of the seed on every side. Minimum size is 16px. Do not rotate it, fill the opening, or put the seed anywhere but in the gap.
